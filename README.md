Online recruitment platforms have changed how individuals discover jobs and boosted job applications, making it harder for recruiters to find and evaluate prospects.
Recruiters receive hundreds of thousands of applications for one position, making manual screening impractical.
Job seekers rarely receive feedback or learn why they were rejected or how well their qualifications met the job requirements.
Traditional resume screening uses keyword searches. They ignore the context and value of experience and reject strong candidates because their resumes don't match
word-for-word.

This project aims to create an AI-based application that empowers candidates and understands semantics rather than keyword-based resume screening to improve system
efficiency.
This helps job seekers see how their resume matches a job and what skills they lack. 
A candidate's résumé is compared to the job description. It interprets text using NLP and semantic similarity models.

From a resume, the algorithm may extract important information and abilities. It will analyse a job description's abilities and requirements, determine how well
the resume matches, and identify any skills that are missing or matching. It will analyse skill gaps, give a match score or rank, and suggest ways the candidate
might improve. This project's key novelty lies in pairing semantic understanding with skill-gap analysis and personalised suggestions.
The platform's multi-tiered analytical methodology precisely breaks down and compares text as follows:
Text extraction and entity recognition: The system automatically categorises resumes and job advertisements based on technical skills, soft skills, domain 
expertise, and educational background.

Dynamics Alignment and Scoring: The algorithm directly compares the candidate's parsed profile to the job description's primary requirements to calculate a
complete match score that measures overall fit.
Targeted Skill-Gap Analysis: The platform separates exact matches and missing qualifications, giving job seekers a quick, detailed insight into their profile 
strengths and weaknesses.
This screening tool differs from others by leveraging generative AI to provide personalised, growth-orientated feedback. First, identify a candidate's skill gap.
Helping them fill gaps is the true value. Instead of passively regulating similarity data, the platform provides effective guidance. It advises candidates to
reframe achievements, reward concealed experience to match industry terminology, or pursue specialised learning courses and certifications to qualify for target
roles.

This platform optimises communication between job seekers and hiring managers by using generative AI, powerful NLP, and a user-friendly user interface.
It turns a confusing and discouraging application procedure into an engaging, interactive and data-driven learning experience. The solution aligns job seekers
with employer expectations better and more transparently, boosting candidate confidence and hiring efficiency.
The research intends to develop and evaluate an intelligent resume-job matching system using NLP, TF-IDF, cosine similarity, and SBERT semantic similarity
to measure the resume-job matching degree and skill or requirement deficiencies. 

Specific aims include:

1. Process and evaluate job descriptions and resumes using NLP.
2. Compare resume data to job requirements using SBERT-based semantic similarity.
3. Match and gap percentages for the computation requirement.
4. To produce user-friendly statistics to measure candidate skills against job criteria.
